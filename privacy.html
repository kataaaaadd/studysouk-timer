Privacy Policy — StudySouk Timer

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

StudySouk Timer does not collect or sell personal identifying information. Basic Discord user IDs and avatars are accessed temporarily in-client solely to display participants in the active study room.

2. Local Storage

Study session preferences, completed Pomodoro counts, and custom tasks are stored locally on your device via standard browser LocalStorage.

3. Third-Party Services

This application operates within the Discord Embedded App framework and adheres strictly to Discord Privacy Policies.

4. Updates

Any updates to this policy will be reflected on this page.
